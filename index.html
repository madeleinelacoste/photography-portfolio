About Me

Hi, I’m Maddie.

I’m drawn to travel, everyday details, warm light, architecture, color, and images that feel a little nostalgic. This is a home for the places and moments I want to remember.

I grew up between Malawi, Tanzania, South Africa, Indonesia, and Seattle before moving to Spain after college. Constantly experiencing new places shaped the way I see the world — and eventually, the way I photograph it. I’m drawn to the small details, colors, architecture, and fleeting moments that make a place feel distinct.
